## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-stakes, competitive atmosphere engineered for custom match tracking (내전 전적 기록). Merging sleek cybernetic HUD motifs with polished glassmorphism, it evokes the prestige and tension of an official esports broadcast tournament stage.

### Brand Personality & Tone
- **Competitive & Authoritative:** Sharp, disciplined, and uncompromising. Every detail reflects match integrity and definitive outcomes.
- **Electric & Focused:** Deep, shadowy environments illuminated by intense neon currents. Visual energy directly mirrors team allegiance (Blue vs. Red) and high stakes.
- **Tactile & Responsive:** Controls and containers feel calibrated and mechanical, evoking advanced Hextech telemetry devices.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
Designed for competitive gaming circles, collegiate clubs, and internal tournament organizers who value rapid record-keeping, high visual polish, and unmistakable match resolution clarity. The interface instills pride in victory, crisp visibility of stakes, and tension in showdowns.

### Visual Style
- **Cybernetic Glassmorphism:** Translucent slate-navy panels hovering over void-level backgrounds, bound by fine-line emissive borders and angular sub-elements.
- **Dichromatic Team Tension:** Electric cobalt/cyan clashes against fierce crimson/ruby across symmetric tournament layouts.
- **Hextech Metallics & Data Nodes:** Accent points leverage antique Hextech gold and luminous cyan to denote value, currency, and confirmed settlements.

## Colors

The palette revolves around deep midnight foundations punctuated by sharp neon team identifiers, Hextech highlights, and structured interface tiers.

### Surface System
- **Void Canvas (`#0B0F19`):** Primary background base; absorbs glow effects.
- **Deep Surface (`#111827`):** Structural backing and lower card wells.
- **Elevated Card Layer (`#131C31`):** Primary interactive card tier.
- **High-State Surface (`#1A2642`):** Focused modules, active modals, and hovered items.
- **Cybernetic Glass Border (`#2A3B60`):** Default low-intensity perimeter border (1px).

### Brand & Value Accents
- **Hextech Cyan (`#0AC8B9`):** Primary interactive actions, confirmed states, and cybernetic indicators. Secondary highlight: `#38BDF8`.
- **Hextech Gold (`#C8AA6E`):** Wager/Stake highlights, match bounty callouts, and victory laurels. Muted tier: `#8C7443`.

### Faction System (Strict Binary)
- **Team Blue (Alliance):**
  - Base Glow / Solid: `#2563EB`
  - Active / Neon: `#3B82F6`
  - Highlight Edge: `#60A5FA`
  - Glow Tint: `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.25)`
- **Team Red (Opponent):**
  - Base Glow / Solid: `#DC2626`
  - Active / Neon: `#EF4444`
  - Highlight Edge: `#F87171`
  - Glow Tint: `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.25)`

### Functional Tones
- **Text Primary:** `#F8FAFC` (100% white-blue clarity)
- **Text Secondary:** `#94A3B8` (System slate)
- **Text Muted:** `#475569` (Structural labels)
- **Error / Void:** `#EF4444`

## Typography

Typography delivers immediate legibility during intense gameplay tracking while honoring modern esports broadcasts and Korean gaming culture.

### Font Roles
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Clean, geometric, and aggressive with a futuristic slant. Applied to match headlines, VS markers, and section titles.
- **Body & Names (`Inter`):** Reliable, high-density sans-serif optimized for Korean and English text rendering. Applied to summoner participant names, form fields, and narrative records.
- **System Data (`JetBrains Mono`):** Precision-engineered monospaced numbers and codes for timestamps, stake values (KRW/points), and slot indices (P1–P5).

### Composition & Casing
- All team badges, stake headers, and status markers (`VICTORY`, `DEFEAT`, `PENDING`) use strict uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.08em` to `0.1em`).
- Monetary and point tallies must consistently align using tabular figures from `JetBrains Mono` to ensure instant scanability across history tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a structured, fluid grid calibrated to display bilateral 5v5 team confrontations with maximum visual balance.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout. Match confrontation modules occupy a full 12-column span split symmetrically: Blue Team (5 cols), Center Verdict & Stakes (2 cols), Red Team (5 cols).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Center verdict scales down to an inline pill between stacked team columns or sits as an absolute header badge.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column layout. Teams stack vertically (Blue Team on top, Red Team below) separated by an interlocking central stake/winner status ribbon.

### Spacing Principles
- Symmetrical inner padding within participant slots ensures name lengths up to 16 characters stay perfectly balanced between Blue and Red rosters.
- Component gap transitions strictly leverage `space-xs` (4px) for micro badges, `space-sm` (8px) between slot inputs, and `space-md` (16px) between thematic containers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use soft natural shadows; instead, it relies on luminous energy states, cybernetic stroke halos, and stepped surface containers.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Background Horizon (0dp):** Solid `#0B0F19` with a subtle static radial grid pattern in `rgba(42, 59, 96, 0.15)`.
2. **Surface Canvas (1dp):** `#111827` with 1px border `#2A3B60`. Used for history logs and inactive record lists.
3. **Active Match Stage (2dp):** `#131C31` enhanced with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and perimeter border `1px solid rgba(42, 59, 96, 0.8)`.
4. **Neon Confrontation Overlay (3dp):** Dynamic team-colored luminescent aura:
   - Blue Victory State: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.4), inset 0 0 12px 0 rgba(10, 200, 185, 0.15)`
   - Red Victory State: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.4), inset 0 0 12px 0 rgba(248, 113, 113, 0.15)`
5. **Modal / Form Focus (4dp):** Deep navy `#1A2642` overlaid with gold cyber-border `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(200, 170, 110, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses a soft-beveled mechanical aesthetic (`roundedness: 1`). Elements favor precise, restrained geometry (4px default corner radii) reminiscent of competitive gaming hardware HUDs, avoiding organic, pillowy curves.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Components (Inputs, Participant Slots, Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`). Imparts structural rigidity and technical precision.
- **Match Cards & Sub-Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `8px` (`0.5rem`). Keeps heavy multi-tiered data cards clean and aligned.
- **Modals & Master Stage Displays (`rounded-xl`):** `12px` (`0.75rem`).
- **Pills / Status Dots:** Strict full rounds (`9999px`) are exclusively reserved for micro live indicators and radio selection nodes.

## Components

Every component adheres strictly to tracking: Match Date, Winner (Blue/Red), Stake, and Participants (5 slots per team).

### Match Summary Card
- **Structure:** Symmetrical horizontal layout (desktop) or stacked format (mobile).
- **Header:** Match Date (`JetBrains Mono`, muted slate) left-aligned; Stake Callout (`Space Grotesk` + `#C8AA6E` gold icon) right-aligned.
- **Matchup Roster:**
  - Blue Team Column: 5 Participant name rows (`Inter`, slate-50). Winner state triggers a subtle `#2563EB` left border line (3px) and faint cyan glow.
  - Center Lockup: "VS" divider or Victory Shield displaying winning team with a high-contrast badge.
  - Red Team Column: 5 Participant name rows. Winner state triggers a `#DC2626` right border line (3px) and ruby glow.
- **Background:** `#131C31` with `#2A3B60` outline.

### Input Fields & Participant Slots
- **Default State:** `#111827` surface, 1px `#2A3B60` border, `#F8FAFC` text, `4px` radius.
- **Participant Input (5 per team):** Numbered index (`01` through `05`) set in `JetBrains Mono` muted cyan for Blue, muted red for Red. Placeholder text: `"소환사명 입력"`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to primary `#0AC8B9` (or team color) with an ambient 4px outer glow ring (`rgba(10, 200, 185, 0.3)`).
- **Error State:** Border shifts to `#EF4444` with helper text rendered in `body-sm` `#F87171`.

### Winner Selector Toggle
- **Design:** Dual-segmented switch spanning the module width.
- **Blue Option:** Hover activates `#2563EB` glow. Selected state fills with linear gradient (`to right, #1E3A8A, #2563EB`) and displays a cyan checkmark with `"BLUE VICTORY"`.
- **Red Option:** Hover activates `#DC2626` glow. Selected state fills with linear gradient (`to left, #7F1D1D, #DC2626`) and displays a ruby checkmark with `"RED VICTORY"`.

### Stake (판돈) Input Module
- **Design:** Numeric input field flanked by quick-tier buttons (+10,000, +50,000, +100,000 KRW / Point presets).
- **Visuals:** Gold Hextech icon prefix (`#C8AA6E`), bold tabular numbers (`data-mono-lg`), and an active subtle amber border under focus (`#C8AA6E`).

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("기록 저장" / Save Match):** Linear gradient from `#0AC8B9` to `#0284C7`, sharp dark text (`#0B0F19`, `font-weight: 700`), hover intensifies cyan glow to 16px spread.
- **Secondary CTA:** Surface `#1A2642` with 1px border `#38BDF8`, hover brightens to `#2A3B60` with primary text `#38BDF8`.
- **Destructive Action:** Ghost surface with `#EF4444` border and text; active glow `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.4)`.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Stake Pill:** `#111827` base, `#C8AA6E` text and border, formatted with currency prefix: `₩ [VALUE]` or `PTS [VALUE]`.
- **Win/Loss Marker:** Sharp, rectangular badge (`roundedness: 1`), uppercase, tracking `0.1em`. Blue: `bg-#1E3A8A text-#60A5FA border-#2563EB`. Red: `bg-#7F1D1D text-#F87171 border-#DC2626`.
